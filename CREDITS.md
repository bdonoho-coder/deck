## Color themes

Palettes come from Omarchy's theme files and are converted to Windows accent colors, wallpapers,
icon packs and Terminal schemes by Deck's own scripts. Only the hex color values are used.

- **Omarchy** by David Heinemeier Hansson and contributors, https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy,
  MIT (`LICENSES/Omarchy-MIT.txt`). Omarchy's wallpaper photos are NOT included.
- **Tokyo Night** by Folke Lemaitre, https://github.com/folke/tokyonight.nvim,
  Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSES/TokyoNight-Apache-2.0.txt`). See `NOTICE`.
- **Catppuccin** (Mocha and Latte), https://github.com/catppuccin/catppuccin,
  MIT (`LICENSES/Catppuccin-MIT.txt`).
- **Nord** by Sven Greb, https://github.com/nordtheme/nord, MIT (`LICENSES/Nord-MIT.txt`).
- **Everforest** by Sainnhe Park, https://github.com/sainnhe/everforest,
  MIT (`LICENSES/Everforest-MIT.txt`).
- **Gruvbox** by Pavel Pertsev, https://github.com/morhetz/gruvbox. The upstream repository has no
  license file; only the color values are used, as published in Omarchy (`LICENSES/Gruvbox.txt`).
- **Amber Lab** is an original Deck theme.

Theme names are used to describe the color scheme only. They belong to their projects.

## Fonts

The "HUD Face" font files in `fonts/hud` are renamed copies of these Google Fonts, all under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 with no Reserved Font Name (`LICENSES/Fonts-OFL-1.1.txt`):
Chakra Petch (Cadson Demak), Exo 2 (Natanael Gama), Rajdhani (Indian Type Foundry),
Oxanium (Severin Meyer), Saira (Omnibus-Type). Modified versions stay under the OFL and may not be
sold on their own.

## App icons

- **Simple Icons**, https://simpleicons.org, CC0 1.0 (`LICENSES/SimpleIcons-CC0-1.0.txt`).
  The logos remain trademarks of their owners; Deck uses them only to label the matching app on
  your own PC. Apps without a Simple Icons logo get a letter tile drawn by Deck.

## Icons, wallpapers and art drawn by Deck

Folder icons, file-type icons, drive and Recycle Bin icons, the Explorer top-area art, the ridge,
contour and glow wallpapers and the Deck logo are generated by Deck's own scripts and are covered by
the Deck license (`LICENSE`).

## Required but not bundled

- **AutoHotkey v2**, https://www.autohotkey.com, GPL-2.0. Runs the picker and hotkeys.
- **Windhawk** by Ramen Software, https://windhawk.net, GPL-3.0, and these mods by m417z

  for them (`windhawk-styles.reg`).
- **Python 3 + Pillow**, optional, for the per-user file-type icons (`file_icons.py`).
